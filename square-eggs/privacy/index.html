Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Square Eggs ("the game") is made by MM4Games (Mohammed Medoukh) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at mm4games2020@gmail.com.

1Information saved on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins and skins), your settings (sound, vibration and difficulty), your nickname and your age group are saved only on your device. We cannot see this information.

At first launch the game asks how old you are, but it only keeps your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) - never your exact age or date of birth. The age group is used only to choose safe settings for you (see sections 3 and 4).

2Global leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

To show the global leaderboard, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. This happens after you choose your nickname and creates a random player ID; no email address, password or real name is needed. Your nickname, total score, number of levels cleared and the skin you use are sent to the leaderboard and are visible to other players. Unity also processes your device's IP address to provide the service.

  • Players under 13 cannot type a nickname: they pick a generated name such as "Happy Panda".
  • Players 13 and over can type a nickname. Common rude words and names with long numbers (like phone numbers) are blocked. Please don't use your real name.
  • If you see a rude or offensive name, tap it in the leaderboard to hide that player on your device or to report the name to us. We review reports and remove offensive names.

3Ads (Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners)

The game shows only optional videos, and only to players aged 13 and over: you choose to watch one to revive after a fall or to unlock a skin. There are no banners and no ads that appear by themselves. Players under 13 never see ads (see below). Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with, currently Unity Ads and Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ads.

These ads are family-friendly: our ad partners are set to show only ads suitable for children (no ads for apps rated for teens or adults, and no casino or gambling ads), and ads for dating apps and ads with sexual or suggestive content are blocked. If you ever see an ad that seems inappropriate, tell us at mm4games2020@gmail.com and we will block it.

  • Players under 13: no ads at all. The game never starts the ad services for them, so no ads are requested and no advertising data is collected from children. Reviving and unlocking skins use coins earned in the game instead.
  • Players 13 to 17: ads are never personalised, and their information is never sold or shared for targeted advertising.
  • Players 18 and over: ads may be personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game first asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad choices. In US states with privacy laws, Settings > Ad choices also lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for personalised ads. You can also limit personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads); on iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we don't receive the information that the ad partners collect. Some US state laws (for example in California) count letting ad partners use your advertising identifier for personalised ads as "selling" or "sharing" personal information. If you live in one of these states, you can opt out at any time in Settings > Ad choices; players under 18 are always opted out.

4Children

Square Eggs is made for everyone, including children. The age question is neutral: it has no default age and doesn't hint at any limit. Players under 13 never see ads, can only pick a generated nickname, sharing to other apps is turned off for them, and a grown-up check (a small math question) appears before anything opens outside the game, such as a website or an email.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in this policy. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings > Delete my data. This deletes everything the game saved on your device and your Unity player account, and removes your nickname from the leaderboard. Your score may remain only as an anonymous entry without your nickname, which can no longer be linked to you.
  • By email: write to mm4games2020@gmail.com with your nickname and we will delete your leaderboard entry and any other data we hold about you within 30 days. You do not need the game installed to do this.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything saved on your device.

To manage data held by ad partners, use your device's ad settings (reset or delete the advertising ID) and the partners' own privacy tools (see section 7).

6How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. Leaderboard data (random player ID, nickname, score, levels cleared and skin) is kept while the leaderboard exists, or until you delete it as described above. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

7Third-party services

  • Unity Gaming Services (anonymous sign-in and leaderboard): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent form: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

8Security

Data sent to Unity Gaming Services and to the ad partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Nothing is stored on servers that we run ourselves.

9Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, California or other US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit how it is used, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising (Settings > Ad choices). To use these rights, use the in-game options above or contact us at mm4games2020@gmail.com. Using them will not change how the game works for you.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
