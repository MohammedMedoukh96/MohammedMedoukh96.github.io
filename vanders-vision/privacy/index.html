Vander's Vision - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Vander's Vision ("the game") is made by MM4Games (Mohammed Medoukh) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at mm4games2020@gmail.com.

1. Information saved on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, bolts, abilities), your settings, your hero name, your ad choice and the age you entered are saved only on your device. We cannot see this information. The age you enter is used only to choose safe settings for you (see section 4).

2. Global leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

To show the global leaderboard, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. This creates a random player ID; no email address, password or real name is needed. Your hero name and your total score are sent to the leaderboard and are visible to other players. If you see a name that is offensive, tap it in the leaderboard to hide it on your device or to report it to us; we review reports and remove offensive names. Unity also processes your device's IP address to provide the service. Nothing is sent before you finish the first-launch setup.

3. Ads (Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners)

The game contains one kind of ad: an optional video you can choose to watch to revive your hero after a fall. There are no banners and no ads that appear by themselves. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (for example Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob). To deliver, measure and limit ads, these partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads.

  • Players under 13: ads are requested as child-directed and are never personalised.
  • Players aged 13 to 15: ads are never personalised.
  • Players 16 and over: you decide whether ads may be personalised - at first launch, and at any time in Settings > Profile > Personalised ads. On iPhone and iPad we only ask for tracking permission if you choose personalised ads, and you can change your answer in the iOS Settings app (Privacy & Security > Tracking).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising unless you choose personalised ads.

4. Children

The game asks for your age so younger players get safer settings: players under 13 cannot type their own name (they pick a generated hero name), ads are child-directed and never personalised, and no tracking permission is requested. The age you enter is not checked. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in this policy. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings > Profile > Delete my data. This deletes everything the game saved on your device, deletes your Unity player account and anonymises your leaderboard name. Any remaining leaderboard record is removed within 30 days.
  • By email: write to mm4games2020@gmail.com with your hero name and we will delete your leaderboard data within 30 days. You do not need the game installed to do this.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything saved on your device.

To manage data held by ad partners, use your device's ad settings (reset or delete the advertising ID) and the partners' own privacy tools.

6. How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. Leaderboard data (player ID, hero name and score) is kept while the leaderboard exists, or until you delete it as described above.

7. Third-party services

  • Unity Gaming Services: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Security

Data sent to Unity Gaming Services and to ad partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Nothing is stored on servers that we run ourselves.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, and to object to or limit how it is used. To use these rights, use the in-game options above or contact us at mm4games2020@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
